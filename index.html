How a campaign runs

Three scenarios, illustrative only.

These are illustrative examples of our process, not completed client campaigns. Real case studies go here once we've run one with you.

Illustrative — Independent Hip-Hop Single

Challenge

An independent US rapper releases a single with no existing audience or team in India.

Strategy

Brief a mix of meme pages and dance creators to turn a specific hook into a repeatable format, timed around the release date.

What we'd track

Content volume delivered, creator reach, and the resulting shift in India streaming numbers pre- vs. post-campaign.

Illustrative — Afrobeats EP Rollout

Challenge

A label wants to test India ahead of a wider Afrobeats EP rollout, with a limited budget for a first pass.

Strategy

Concentrate on fitness and dance creators, where Afrobeats already fits existing content formats natively.

What we'd track

Engagement rate by creator category, to inform which categories get budget in the next phase.

Illustrative — EDM Festival Anthem

Challenge

An EDM producer's festival-circuit track needs a foothold in India before a potential tour date.

Strategy

Lifestyle and fashion creators built around a visual identity tied to the track, plus a coordinated posting window.

What we'd track

Saves/shares as a leading indicator, plus geo-tagged listener growth in India specifically.
